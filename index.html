How it works

  1. Write the words

    A brand name, a headline, a subheadline and a call to action — or let a one-line prompt suggest them for you.

  2. Pick a template and a palette

    Five motion styles, four theme presets, or set every color by hand.

  3. Generate and download

    Thumbstop renders the animation to your canvas in real time and records it straight to a video file you can post anywhere.
